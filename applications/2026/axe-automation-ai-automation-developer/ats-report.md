## 3. Hidden requirements and hiring signals

### Hidden requirements
1. **Portfolio / Loom walkthrough expected.** Automation agencies almost
   always ask for a recorded walkthrough of a real workflow or a portfolio.
   The candidate has ugo-landing.vercel.app + GitHub — recommend preparing a
   2–3 min Loom of the AI lead-qualification workflow (n8n + Claude +
   Airtable) before submitting. High-leverage, low-cost.
2. **Client-facing communication in fluent English.** Daily calls + client
   delivery imply spoken-English screening; likely a video interview or async
   video question early in the funnel.
3. **Timezone overlap.** "Anywhere in the world" but daily calls means real
   synchronous overlap, almost certainly US business hours (US-based agency).
   WAT (GMT+1) overlaps US East afternoons comfortably (2pm WAT = 8/9am ET) —
   state this proactively; it removes a silent rejection reason.
4. **Speed + ambiguity tolerance.** "Hyper-growth", 3-year-old agency,
   "motivated" — expect vague specs, shifting priorities, multiple projects in
   parallel. Mirror with "translated business requirements into scalable
   automation solutions" (already in resume).
5. **Likely paid test project or trial task** (standard for this agency
   class; their own marketing sells "$29/hour AI developers", so they run a
   volume funnel with practical filters).
6. **Rate sensitivity.** The "$29/hour" productized offer on their site
   signals cost-conscious hiring, likely geo-flexible compensation. Set salary
   expectations accordingly against `config/career.yaml` floor before investing
   further.
