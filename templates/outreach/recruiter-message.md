# Recruiter Message — structure (max 150 words)

> Structure only — never send templated text.

1. Greeting (name, correct spelling)
2. Personalization — one specific fact about the company or role
3. Why you applied — one sentence connecting your background to their problem
4. One relevant achievement — with a true metric, from the master resume
5. CTA — low-friction ("happy to share a quick demo" / "open to a short call")

Rules: no flattery filler, no resume dump, no pressure, no "I'm a hard worker".
The message should make replying easy and worthwhile.
