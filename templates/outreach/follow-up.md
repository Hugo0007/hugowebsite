# Follow-up Cadence

Day 3 — short nudge: one sentence, reference the application, no guilt-tripping.
Day 7 — add value: share something relevant (demo link, useful observation
        about their stack/product, article you wrote). This is the message
        that earns replies.
Day 14 — graceful final: close the loop politely, leave the door open.
After day 14 — archive the thread. Never message again about this opening
        (a future opening is a fresh, natural reason to reconnect).

Never send two unanswered messages less than 3 days apart. Never send
identical follow-ups to different people.
